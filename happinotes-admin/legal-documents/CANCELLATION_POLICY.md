# HappiNotes Cancellation Policy

**Review draft — not approved for publication.**

Cancel through the store account used to subscribe: Google Play subscription settings for an Android purchase or Apple subscription settings for an iOS purchase. Uninstalling the app, signing out, or requesting HappiNotes account deletion does not cancel store billing. The store confirms cancellation and the date Premium access ends. Source code indicates cancellation state is synchronized from the provider; exact timing and supported recovery cases need release review.

Cancellation generally stops the next renewal; access may continue through the paid period shown by the store. Local mandatory cancellation, cooling-off and refund rights prevail. Do not promise immediate cancellation, a particular refund, or a specific deadline unless required and confirmed for the customer’s market.

For access or account help, contact hello@happinotes.online. Never email passwords, OTPs or purchase tokens.
