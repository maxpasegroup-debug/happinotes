# Copyright and Content Complaint Procedure

**Internal review draft — legal roles and deadlines unresolved.**

1. Receive claims at hello@happinotes.online and record a unique case reference, received time, affected URL/content, claimant and alleged right.
2. A trained reviewer checks completeness, rights evidence and whether immediate temporary action is warranted under approved law and policy.
3. Preserve only necessary evidence under restricted access. Do not promise removal or restoration until a decision is made.
4. Record the decision, reasons, reviewer and notice to affected parties. Escalate disputed ownership, counter-notices and statutory notices to counsel.
5. Close only after the decision and any required follow-up are recorded.

No actual ticketing system or formal legal escalation role was found in source. Counsel must define required notices, deadlines, counter-notice and repeat-infringer process; owner must appoint an accountable contact before this is used as an operational SOP.
