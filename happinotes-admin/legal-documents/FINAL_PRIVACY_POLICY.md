## Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, HappiNotes may handle:

- **Account and sign-in information:** name, email address and/or phone number, a protected password verifier, account identifier, language and other account settings, and authentication/session information.
- **Security and verification information:** one-time verification codes and delivery details, password-reset and reauthentication records, and information used to protect accounts and process requests.
- **Library and preferences:** favourites, book collections, purchased-book references, language and theme preferences, and the account's current Premium or subscription access state.
- **Store transaction information:** store, product or plan identifiers, transaction references, purchase and subscription state, expiry or renewal state where supplied by the store, and information used to verify store notifications. Google Play or Apple controls checkout and payment details submitted to it. HappiNotes does not ask you to send us your store password or payment-card number.
- **Deletion-request information:** a request reference, date, source, status, processing history, and the information needed to verify and handle the request. If the website gives you a private status code, keep it safe.
- **Notification information:** push-notification device token references and the content of service notifications.
- **Books and media:** catalogue and library information and the audio, image or video media needed to provide the service.
- **Device and browser information:** session credentials, app settings, cached catalogue or book information, and browser-stored session, playback-completion or deletion-request information. Some information remains on your device or browser until you clear it.
- **Operational information:** information generated when the service is accessed, such as application, hosting, security and access logs. The exact information collected by hosting and other infrastructure services depends on their configuration.

## Account deletion and current limitations

You can start a deletion request from the signed-in app or through the public [account-deletion page](https://happinotes.online/account-deletion). The in-app process requires an authenticated session and a further identity check. The website process verifies control of the account's registered email using a one-time code. External verification using only a phone number is not available. The website provides a request reference and private status code; save that code securely because it may not be possible to recover it after account contact details are removed.

After a request is accepted for processing, the service removes password-reset and reauthentication records and matching verification codes, invalidates the account session, clears favourites, collections and push-token references, replaces the profile name with a generic label, resets selected settings, removes email and phone, and removes HappiNotes access to purchased books and Premium features.

These steps do not mean the request is complete. Some account information and the account identifier remain linked to purchase and subscription transaction records while their disposition is reviewed. Support communications, provider-side records, operational logs, backups and deletion-request history are not all removed through the account-deletion process. A request may pause while remaining records and actions are reviewed. We do not promise a fixed completion time. A request is complete only when the service confirms completion; “request received” or “under review” is not confirmation that all information has been deleted.

Deleting a HappiNotes account does not cancel recurring billing in Google Play or Apple. Cancel separately in the relevant store to stop future renewals.
