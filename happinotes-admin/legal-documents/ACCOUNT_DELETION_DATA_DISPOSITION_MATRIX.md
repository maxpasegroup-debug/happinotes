# HappiNotes — Account Deletion Data Disposition Matrix

**Internal engineering and legal/finance review draft — not an approved retention schedule.**  
**Company:** AIRASKILL CITY PRIVATE LIMITED  
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-04  
**Purpose:** Map repository data fields to the proposed deletion treatment, actual worker behavior, dependencies, and implementation needed. This is not a legal opinion. No retention period or legal ground is created by this matrix.

## Reading this matrix

- **Proposed target** describes the requested privacy-first architecture.
- **Current behavior** describes local source code, not production provider configuration.
- **Decision needed** identifies where counsel, finance, tax, or the owner must determine the applicable rule.
- A record must not be treated as an approved retention exception unless its exact fields, purpose, legal provision (if established), trigger, period or event criterion (if established), access controls, and eventual disposal method have been documented and approved.
- `deferred_policy` is a pause, not a retention exception and not a successful deletion checkpoint.

## Retention exception record required before activation

For each field retained after a deletion request, record: (1) exact fields; (2) business purpose; (3) legal provision and subsection, if established; (4) applicability facts; (5) retention trigger and period/event, if established; (6) authorized access roles; (7) separation/pseudonymisation method; (8) post-expiry deletion method; (9) approving owner, counsel and finance reviewer; and (10) evidence/review date. A missing item remains `deferred_policy`; it cannot be converted to `complete` merely to unblock the worker.

## Evidence and design sources

- Google Play requires an in-app deletion path and an external web resource when accounts can be created. Its guidance permits specified retention for legitimate reasons only when disclosed and says providers should be asked to delete data processed on the developer’s behalf. [Account deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111), [User Data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311).
- Spotify’s privacy policy is a structural example of explaining data categories, purposes, sharing, retention, transfers, security and user controls. HappiNotes must not copy Spotify’s purposes, legal bases or retention periods. [Spotify Privacy Policy](https://www.spotify.com/in-en/legal/privacy-policy/).
- Audible India’s guide illustrates that closing an Audible account is coupled with Amazon account closure and loss of library access. HappiNotes should make the consequences of account deletion and separate store billing explicit; it must not copy Audible’s model or retention terms. [Audible India personal data guide](https://help.audible.in/s/article/manage-your-personal-data).
- Indian Companies Act §128(5) concerns company books of account and relevant vouchers; CGST Act §§35–36 concern specified registered-person accounts and records. Applicability to each HappiNotes field is unresolved. The cited CBIC consolidation is amended only through 31 August 2021 and expressly has no legal binding force; it is a pointer for counsel, not a current-law conclusion. See [India Code Companies Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2114/5/A2013-18.pdf?v=20260603082842) and [CBIC CGST Act consolidation](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/CGST-Act-Updated-31082021.pdf).
- DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025 have staged commencement under Gazette notifications. Counsel must confirm effective provisions and applicability against current official notices. See [Act commencement notification](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267647.pdf) and [Rules 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).

## Current release consequence

The account request is accepted and access is blocked. The worker clears authentication artifacts, preferences/device references, ordinary profile/contact fields, and HappiNotes entitlements before it pauses at `blocked_policy`. Purchase/subscription evidence and support/provider/log/backup/audit handling remain unresolved. It cannot be represented as complete.
