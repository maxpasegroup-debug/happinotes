## Retention and deletion

Specific retention periods and legal grounds have not been approved. Users can request deletion in the signed-in app or at [Account deletion](/account-deletion). The external request form verifies control of an account email; phone-only external verification is not implemented. The current worker removes OTP/reset artifacts and clears collections, favourites and push tokens, then pauses at a policy gate. It retains the profile, purchase records, subscription and entitlement and does not mark the request complete. Accordingly, the current workflow is not full account/data deletion. The policy and Play declarations must be updated after the approved deletion process is implemented.
