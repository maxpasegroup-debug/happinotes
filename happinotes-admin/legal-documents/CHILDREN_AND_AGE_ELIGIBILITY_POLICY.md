# Children and Age Eligibility

**Decision pending — do not represent a target age as confirmed.**

The source audit did not establish whether HappiNotes is intended for children, whether children may create accounts, whether the app is child-directed, or whether age/parental-consent controls exist. The owner must select intended audience and minimum age with counsel, and make the Play Console target audience, store listing, content, privacy notice and actual product behavior consistent.

If the service is directed to or knowingly collects personal information from children, additional rules may apply, including parental consent and restrictions on collection, use, disclosure and retention. Applicable age thresholds differ by market. Do not rely on a generic “not for children” sentence to resolve product or legal obligations. Source: [FTC COPPA rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/federal-register-notices/16-cfr-part-312-coppa-final-rule-amendments); [India DPDP Act and Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025%3B).
