# HappiNotes Subscription Terms

**Review draft — not approved for publication.**

Subscriptions provide Premium access to eligible HappiNotes content. Android transactions use Google Play Billing; iOS transactions use Apple In-App Purchase. Store availability and account eligibility apply. Each store presents the final localized amount, currency, billing frequency, renewal date/amount and any introductory offer before purchase. Payment credentials and recurring charges are handled by the store. The backend verifies purchases before granting or updating Premium access.

The planned India monthly reference price is **₹1,999** and offer price **₹699**. The mathematical discount against ₹1,999 is **65.03%**. These are planned values and are not evidence of live store prices. The current business assumption is ₹699 recurs each month for the subscriber who joins at that offer, without a later automatic switch to ₹1,999. This depends on actual store product/base-plan setup and must be verified in both consoles before advertising.

The owner intends existing active subscribers to keep their price while continuously subscribed; changes should affect new or returning subscribers after expiry. Store rules can require notices or consent for price changes and can limit how cohorts/products are configured. Confirm price-version mapping, product identifiers, migrations, notification processing and store configuration. Cancellation stops future renewal according to the relevant store; access generally remains until the store-reported expiry. Restore purchases is available in-app. Account deletion does not cancel a store subscription.

Taxes, introductory pricing, cooling-off rights and refunds vary by market and store. The checkout price is authoritative for the customer. India GST treatment and all legal/finance wording require professional approval. Do not publish the proposed amounts as live until the stores confirm them.
