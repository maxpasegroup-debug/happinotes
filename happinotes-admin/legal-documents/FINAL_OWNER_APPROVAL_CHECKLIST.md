# Final Owner Approval Checklist

**Status: unresolved items block legal publication and public release.**

## Owner decisions

- [ ] Confirm intended countries/territories at launch and actual Play availability.
- [ ] Confirm target age/audience and whether children can create accounts; align Play target audience and product safeguards.
- [ ] Confirm the complete production provider/subprocessor list, account owners, data regions, contracts and retention settings.
- [ ] Approve purposes and operational owner for each data category.
- [ ] Appoint accountable privacy, security, support and statutory grievance contacts; only publish names/designations after confirmation.
- [ ] Approve actual live prices/products in each country and confirm ₹699 recurring offer cohort behavior, price changes and returning-subscriber behavior in Play Console.
- [ ] Confirm subscription cancellation, access expiry, restore, refunds and customer support handling.
- [ ] Obtain finance/tax advice on India GST and other markets; checkout price must match store configuration.
- [ ] Confirm catalogue rights, territories, licenses, claims and copyright complaint authority.

## Legal and finance approvals

- [ ] Approve lawful grounds, notices, consumer disclosures, rights process, response times and international transfer clauses by launch market.
- [ ] Approve record-by-record retention periods or criteria, legal bases, restricted access, deletion/anonymisation fields and accounting exceptions.
- [ ] Approve backup/log expiry, vendor deletion instructions and minimal deletion-request history period.
- [ ] Approve cancellation/refund and digital-content consumer rights per market.
- [ ] Review incident notification rules and approve incident contacts/escalation.
- [ ] Set effective dates and authorize publication of each legal notice.

## Engineering / Play blockers

- [ ] Implement approved deletion to completion, including provider-side actions, entitlements, store linkage, backups/logs and auditable status. Keep the gate until approved.
- [ ] Resolve phone-only external request verification if the supported account model requires it.
- [ ] Verify actual web routes and in-app disclosures in release version.
- [ ] Complete final SDK/data-flow review and matching Play Data safety/Data deletion declarations.
- [ ] Verify Play product setup, price, markets, restore, cancellation and review access.
- [ ] Conduct the separately authorized verification/release workflow on the exact candidate build before upload.
