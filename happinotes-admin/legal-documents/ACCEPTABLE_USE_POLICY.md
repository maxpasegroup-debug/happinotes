# HappiNotes Acceptable Use Policy

**Review draft — owner and counsel approval required.**

Use HappiNotes lawfully and for personal access as allowed by the applicable subscription or purchase. Do not:

- share account credentials or evade purchase, subscription or access checks;
- copy, scrape, record, redistribute or publicly perform catalogue media without permission;
- disrupt, probe, reverse engineer or bypass security except where mandatory law permits;
- upload or transmit unlawful, infringing, deceptive, abusive or malicious material through any feature that permits it;
- misuse support, deletion, OTP or purchase-verification processes.

The current source appears primarily to serve company-curated content and does not establish a user-generated-content publishing feature. Suspension criteria, notice/appeal process and complaint handling require approval. This policy does not restrict non-waivable rights.
